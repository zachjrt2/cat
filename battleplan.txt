DESIGN DECISIONS
----------------
- All sanctuary cats imported as army (kittens, teens, adults all join)
- Life stage scaling: Adults = 1.0x (2× teens), Teens = 0.50x (2× kittens), Kittens = 0.25x
- 10 Independent Vertical Lane Queues: cats line up backwards in their specific lane (e.g. 300 cats = 30 in queue per lane)
- Pure vertical queue movement: queues only move vertically forward within their lane as cats fall in that specific lane
- 🔊 Conquest Mode Audio:
  • High-energy BGM (music2.mp3) plays continuously while Conquest Mode is active
  • Clean snappy pop SFX plays whenever any cat is defeated (KO)
  • Ambient background SFX (sanctuary meows, chirps, purrs, hunger distress) are muted while in Conquest Mode
  • Ambient sanctuary music (music.mp3) automatically resumes upon exiting Conquest Mode
- 3× Battle Speed: fast 230ms combat rounds, snappy attack lunges, quick KO poofs, and seamless queue advancing
- 🧬 Mutation Combat Passives:
  • 🔥 Flaming: Burns enemy lane on hit (damage over time)
  • ❄️ Frosted: Chills & slows enemy lane attack rate
  • 😇 Angelic: Halo pulses periodic +20 HP heals to all reserve cats in queue behind
  • 🦨 Stinky: Stench cloud gives enemies 35% miss chance
  • 🦣 Giant: Cleave quake attacks hit target + adjacent lanes with arena screen shake
  • 🐾 Tiny: +30% evasion & counter-attacks on dodge
  • ✨ Gilded / Sparkly: Releases Golden Legacy (+40 HP shield) to next cat on defeat
  • 🌀 Inverted / Chromatic: Prismatic reflection reflects 25% incoming damage
- Dance Item Economy Rebalance (Linear 1..7 ⭐/cat):
  • 🎶 Party Whistle (Conga Line): +1 ⭐ per cat
  • 🌧️ Rainmaker Bell (Rain Dance): +2 ⭐ per cat
  • ❄️ Snowflake Crystal (Snowflake Mandala): +3 ⭐ per cat
  • 💖 Catnip Heart Wand (Heart Formation): +4 ⭐ per cat
  • ♾️ Infinity Metronome (Infinity Loop): +5 ⭐ per cat
  • 🌅 Solar Prism (Sunset Spiral): +6 ⭐ per cat
  • 🧭 Star Compass (Cat Constellation): Costs 5,000 ⭐ Stars in Shop; yields +7 ⭐ per cat, +1,600 💗 & instantly advances the life stage of every participating cat (Kitten → Teen → Adult)!
  • 🛡️ Conquest UI & Aesthetics:
    • Custom clean vector swords SVG icon on shop tab, conquest header, and action buttons
    • Cleaned all extraneous emoji icons from conquest tab and scene, keeping only the dedicated conquest SVG icon
    • 10 combat columns with pure vertical queue ranks
    • Clean pixel-art friendly HP bars with dynamic status badges (removed the blue shield bar overlay)






- Clean KO removal: defeated cats play a poof effect and disappear immediately with no lingering ghosts
- Rare indicator rings / halos scaled down to half size for clean visuals
- Hordes scale linearly across 10 regions: 30 → 60 → 90 → 120 → 150 → 180 → 210 → 240 → 270 → 300 cats (Boss Battle)

- Authentic pixel-art cat canvas animations matching sanctuary styles (including mutations & stage sizes)
- Visuals completely match Cozy Cat Sanctuary (wood plank header, cream parchment, green courtyard arena, cobblestone clash divider)
- Conquest progress (cleared regions) persisted to localStorage
- Entry point: "⚔️ Conquest" tab in Shop modal, costs 💗 Care Points
- Battle runs at smooth tick rate with attack lunges, impact flashes, and KO poofs
- Conqueror badge (clear all 10 regions) is cosmetic/prestige only

====================================================
  STAT DERIVATION TABLE
====================================================

Base stats (all cats):
  HP        = 50 + happiness * 0.5       (range: 50–100)
  Attack    = 15
  Defense   = 10
  Speed     = energy * 0.8 + 10          (range: 10–90)
  CritChance= 0.05 (5% base)
  Evasion   = 0.05 (5% base)

Major trait bonuses:
  hunter/zoomie     → +20 Attack
  cuddler/social    → +20 Defense
  diva              → +15 Defense, +10 HP
  lazy              → +25 HP (tanky)

Minor trait bonuses:
  mischievous       → +15% crit chance
  shy               → +10% evasion

Age bonus:
  +1 to all stats per 7 ageDays

Rarity multiplier:
  isRare            → all stats × 1.30

Mutation modifiers:
  giant             → HP × 1.50
  flaming           → Attack × 1.30
  frosted           → Defense × 1.30
  sparkly           → all × 1.15
  angelic           → HP × 1.25, Defense × 1.25
  gilded            → all × 1.20
  tiny              → Speed × 1.40, HP × 0.70
  chromatic         → CritChance +0.20
  inverted          → Evasion +0.15

Rare type multiplier:
  golden            → all × 1.50 (Legendary tier)
  royal             → all × 1.35
  others            → (covered by isRare × 1.30)


====================================================
  SPECIAL ABILITIES (per major trait)
====================================================

hunter      → Pounce:     Deal 3× attack to one random enemy
zoomie      → Zoom Blitz: Hit all enemies for 0.5× attack
social      → Rally Cry:  +20 attack to all allies for 3 rounds
cuddler     → Comfort:    Restore 40 HP to lowest-HP ally
diva        → Taunt:      All enemies target this cat for 2 rounds
mischievous → Sabotage:   −40% attack on one enemy for 2 rounds
shy         → Vanish:     Untargetable for 1 round
lazy        → Cat Nap:    Skip 1 round, restore to full HP
curious     → Scout:      +25% damage from all allies for 2 rounds
